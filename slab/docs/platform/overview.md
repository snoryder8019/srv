# Overview

Slab is an all-in-one business platform. One subscription gives you a branded public website plus a private admin workspace for running the business behind it — clients, invoicing, scheduling, marketing, and AI content help.

## What You Get

- **Your website** — branded public site with a landing page, blog, portfolio, custom pages, and a marketplace
- **Admin workspace** — everything managed from `/admin`
- **Clients & CRM** — inquiries, client records, onboarding forms, help requests
- **Meetings & booking** — video meetings with AI notes, plus a public booking page
- **Finance** — invoices, payments, ledger and P&L, calculators, analytics
- **Marketing** — email campaigns, social publishing, QR codes and a digital card, print studio
- **AI agents** — assistants that draft content, design, and admin work for you
- **Team access** — invite collaborators and control exactly what each one can open

Not every tool above is switched on for every workspace. See [Admin Panel](admin-panel.md) for how features become visible.

## How It Works

Your site runs on a subdomain (e.g. `yourbrand.madladslab.com`) or your own custom domain. Each workspace has its own database, its own file storage, and its own encrypted credentials. No other business on the platform can see your data.

The base plan is **$39.95/month**. Paying for a longer window costs less per month.

| Plan | You pay | Access granted | Effective per month |
|------|---------|----------------|---------------------|
| **Free Trial** | $0.00 | 14 days | — |
| **Monthly** | $39.95 | 30 days | $39.95 |
| **Quarterly** | $110.26 | 90 days | $36.75 (8% off) |

Notes on billing:

- Each plan is a **one-time charge that unlocks a set number of days**. There is no silent auto-renewal — you pay again to extend.
- Both **Stripe** and **PayPal** are accepted at checkout.
- The free trial is **one per workspace**. Once used, going live requires a paid plan.
- If you signed up through a referral link from a Slab delegate, your trial may be extended beyond the standard 14 days.

## The sLab Network

The Network is a shared public hub listing the brands built on Slab. Joining is optional and free.

- Turn it on at **Settings → Join the sLab Network**
- Members appear in the public brand directory
- You then choose what to syndicate to the hub: job postings, press releases, blog posts and newsletters, marketplace listings, and YouTube uploads
- Visitors can follow your newsletter directly from the hub
- In exchange, a small "Part of the sLab Network" link appears in your site footer

You can opt out at any time, and you can join the directory without syndicating any content.

## Delegate & Referral Program

Slab has an independent sales-delegate program. Delegates promote Slab, sign up new businesses with a personal referral code, and earn commission on what those businesses pay.

| Year of the referred subscription | Commission |
|-----------------------------------|-----------|
| Year 1 | 40% |
| Year 2 | 20% |
| Year 3 and beyond | 10% |

- Commission is a share of net revenue, paid while the referred workspace stays subscribed
- Delegates sign up at `/delegates/signup` and work from their own portal — referral QR code, lead tracker, sales sheets, and payout history
- Tax details are required before any payout is released (W-9 for US delegates, W-8BEN otherwise); earnings accrue in the meantime
- Payouts are sent via PayPal

## Getting Started

1. **Log in** — visit `/admin/login` and sign in
2. **Set up your brand** — fill in your business profile under Settings & Keys; the AI agents read it
3. **Customize your site** — use Design & Copy for colors, fonts, sections, and text
4. **Add content** — write blog posts, add portfolio items, build pages
5. **Connect integrations** — add payment, email, and Google credentials in Settings
6. **Go live** — start the free trial or choose a plan, then point your domain
