# Print Studio

Design print-ready marketing materials at `/admin/print-studio`.

Print Studio works in **campaigns**. You write your copy and pick a look once, and it flows into every format you've enabled — business cards, flyers, posters, stickers, and more. You then download print-ready files and take them to any printer.

| Postcard | 6 × 4 |
| Flyer — Half | 5.5 × 8.5 |
| Flyer — Letter | 8.5 × 11 |
| Poster — Tabloid | 11 × 17 |
| Poster — Large | 18 × 24 |
| Poster — Big | 24 × 36 |
| Sticker — Round | 3 × 3 |
| Sticker — Square | 3 × 3 |
| Button | 2.25 × 2.25 |
| Button | 1 × 1 |
| Door Hanger | 4.25 × 11 |
| Table Tent | 4 × 6 |
| Yard Sign | 24 × 18 |

Everything is designed at 300 DPI with proper bleed and safe margins, and exports at trim size plus bleed — so a business card comes out at 3.75 × 2.25 inches. All formats are single-sided.

**Each format only shows the fields it has room for.** A business card carries your logo, tagline, contact details, and QR code but never your headline. A one-inch button carries only a logo and QR code. Write your copy once and each piece takes what fits.

## Building a Campaign

### 1. Pick a theme

One theme applies to the whole campaign, bringing its own font pairing and its own way of using your brand colours:

**Classic**, **Art Deco**, **Punk Zine**, **Old English**, **Pencil Sketch**, **Black & White**, **Greyscale**, **Minimal**, **Bold Poster**, and **Retro**.

### 2. Write your copy

Six fields, shared across every format: **Headline**, **Subhead**, **Body**, **Offer / Badge**, **Call to Action**, and **Tagline**.

**Fill with AI** writes all six from a short brief such as "weekend 20% off sale". It uses your brand details and researches the web as it goes.

### 3. Add brand assets

Choose whether to show your logo and which version to use, and pick which of your QR links to embed along with a caption for it. Create one under [QR Codes & Card](qr-codes.md) first if you haven't.

Your business name, phone, email, location, website, logo, and colours are all pulled in automatically from your brand settings.

### 4. Choose a background

Theme default, a solid colour, a gradient, one of your brand images, or an **AI texture**.

AI textures are abstract patterns only — no text, logos, or objects — generated to harmonise with your brand colours. They are produced at a modest resolution, so they work well on small pieces but may look soft stretched across a large poster.

Image and AI backgrounds automatically get a subtle dark gradient behind text so it stays readable.

### 5. Arrange each format

Select any element on the preview to drag, resize, and restyle it — position, width, font size, opacity, layering, alignment, colour, font, or hide it entirely. There are 16 fonts to choose from, and **+ Image** adds extra images from your asset library.

Layouts are saved per format, so a poster and a business card can be arranged completely differently. **Reset layout** returns a format to its default arrangement.

## Downloading

| Action | What you get |
|--------|--------------|
| **PDF** | The current format as a print-ready PDF |
| **PNG** | The current format as a 300 DPI image |
| **Download ZIP (all)** | Every enabled format in one archive |
| **Gang ▸ Letter / Tabloid** | Many copies on one sheet with crop marks |

**Gang sheets** fit as many copies of a piece as will go on a letter or tabloid page, complete with crop marks for cutting. Useful for business cards and stickers when your printer charges by the sheet.

Slab produces the files — there is no ordering, checkout, or print vendor built in. Take the downloads to whichever printer you like.

## Before You Print

- Fill in your business name, phone, email, and location in Settings, or the contact block comes out empty.
- Upload a primary logo under Design, or the logo area stays blank.
- Check your colours under Design, since every theme builds its palette from them.
- Preview each format before downloading the full pack — copy that fits a poster may crowd a business card.
- Ask your printer what they need. The PDFs include bleed, which is what most commercial printers expect.

## Finding It

Print Studio is an **advanced** tool. If you don't see it in the sidebar, switch on **Advanced** at the bottom of the menu. It can also be hidden entirely from **Settings → Slab Functions**.
