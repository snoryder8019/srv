# Notes

Capture and route notes at `/admin/notes`.

Notes is a catch-all inbox for thinking out loud — a client conversation, a half-formed idea, a to-do. Write or dictate it once, then send it wherever it needs to go: a blog draft, a marketing email, social captions, website copy, a client record, or a help request.

## Writing a Note
Type a title and body, or hold the microphone button and talk — dictation runs in your browser and fills the note as you speak. Your browser will ask for microphone permission the first time.

Every note can carry:

- **Tags** — comma-separated, for filtering later
- **A client** — binds the note to a client record

## Short Notes and Long Notes
Slab sizes each note as you save it. Anything up to 60 words stays a short note. Anything longer is treated as a long-form note: the full text is filed away and Slab writes a one or two sentence summary so the list stays readable.

You don't choose — it happens on save, and the note itself reads the same either way.

## TL;DR
Press **TLDR** on any note and Slab produces a plain-language summary in two forms — a short paragraph and a bullet list — plus a handful of suggested tags. Switch between the two with the tabs.

Both are written back in your own voice rather than as commentary on what you said. Tap either one to correct it; edits are saved.

## Tags
Six core tags ship with every workspace and can't be deleted:

`business` · `personal` · `ideas` · `tasks` · `finance` · `random`

Add your own tags from the tag panel. Custom tag names use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens. Each tag shows how many notes carry it, and custom tags can be deleted.

Filter the note list by tag, by client, or by searching the title, body, and summary.

## Notes and Clients
There are two ways a note ends up on a client's record, and both show in the same merged log on the client's Notes tab.

**Bind it.** Pick a client from the dropdown on the note. The note stays a full note — tagged, summarised, distributable — and appears on that client's Notes tab as an authored note. Deleting it here deletes it there too.

**Push a copy.** Use the **Client Note** distribution target to attach a copy of the note's summary to a client. The copy keeps a link back to the original. Removing it from the client detaches the copy only; the note itself stays in Notes.

Notes written from the client's own Notes tab are bound notes — they are saved here and attached to that client automatically.

You can also open Notes already filtered to one client from a link on their record.

## Distributing a Note
Open a note and pick one or more destinations under **Send this note to…**

| Destination | What you get |
|-------------|--------------|
| Blog Post | A full blog draft — title, excerpt, and body — waiting in your blog |
| Email | A marketing email draft with subject and preview text |
| Campaign | Social captions for Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn, saved as a preset |
| Web Copy | Polished website copy written into a section of your site |
| Client Note | A copy of the note attached to a client record |
| Help Request | A support request opened from the note |

Web Copy needs the section you're writing into. Client Note needs a client. The rest work straight away.

Each destination reports back whether it succeeded and where the result landed, and the note keeps a record of everything it has been sent to.

## Re-Sending a Note
Send the same note to the same destination again and Slab **updates** what it made the first time rather than creating a second copy. A blog draft refreshes without changing its address or unpublishing it, a sent campaign isn't reset, and a resolved help request isn't reopened.
