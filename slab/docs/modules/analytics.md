# Analytics

See how your business is doing at `/admin/analytics`.

Analytics pulls activity from across your whole admin — content, email, invoicing, clients, and operations — into one page, and adds live figures straight from your connected social and payment accounts.

## Choosing a Period
Switch between **Today**, **Week**, and **Month** at the top right. Most figures count only what happened inside that window. A few are running totals — total contacts, total clients, unpaid and overdue invoices — because those describe where you stand right now, not what changed.

## Analytics Agent
Ask a plain-English question about your numbers and get a written answer — "how did email perform this week?", "what's our biggest concern?". The agent reads the same figures shown on the page along with your brand details. It only reads; it can't change anything.

## Social Insights
Live figures pulled from the social accounts you've connected, loaded a moment after the page opens. Accounts you haven't connected simply don't appear. See the Social doc for connecting accounts.

## Live Payments
Real transactions pulled directly from Stripe and PayPal over the selected period, charted by day with a split between the two providers.

This is money that actually moved, which is different from the invoice figures further down the page — it includes payments taken outside of an invoice. If a provider isn't set up in Settings it's skipped. PayPal also needs **Transaction Search** enabled on your PayPal app before it will report anything.

## What's Counted
| Section | Shows |
|---------|-------|
| Content Created | New blog posts, pages, sections, portfolio items, and assets |
| Email Marketing | Drafts created, campaigns sent, opens, clicks, bounces, unsubscribes, and inbound replies |
| Revenue & Invoicing | New invoices, invoices paid, revenue collected, plus unpaid and overdue counts |
| Audience | New and total contacts, how many are subscribed, and new and total clients |
| Operations | New meetings, inquiries, support tickets, onboarding forms, and form responses |
| Top Activity | Your most recent campaigns sent and blog posts created |

## Getting the Detail Behind a Number
Analytics is a summary — it points you at what to look into, not the full story.

- Revenue and invoicing figures come from Bookkeeping, where you can filter by status, provider, and client.
- For accounts, budgets, and a real profit & loss statement, use Ledger & P&L.
- Campaign-level opens and clicks live in Email Marketing.

## Reading the Numbers
- **Revenue Paid** counts invoices marked paid during the period. It won't match Live Payments exactly — that one reports every transaction your gateways processed.
- **Overdue** and **Unpaid** are current standing, so they don't change when you switch periods.
- Opens and clicks come from email tracking, which needs images enabled in the recipient's mail app. Treat them as a floor, not an exact count.
- A quiet section usually means a quiet period, not a broken metric. Widen the range to Month to confirm.
